Account & Profile

How do I create a Zarkili account?

Open the Zarkili app and tap Sign Up. You can register with:

  • Apple ID — fastest option on iPhone
  • Google account
  • Email & password

You can also book as a guest without an account — just enter your name, email, and phone when prompted.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email address. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

If you signed up with Apple or Google, password reset does not apply — use your Apple ID or Google account to sign in.

How do I update my name, phone number, or profile photo?
  • Go to Profile (bottom right tab)
  • Tap Edit Profile
  • Update your details and tap Save
How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Go to Profile → Settings → Account
  2. Tap Delete Account
  3. Confirm the deletion

Your personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. Booking and payment records required for legal compliance may be retained for up to 7 years in anonymised form.

Alternatively, email support@zarkili.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Bookings

How do I book an appointment?
  1. Browse the Discover feed or search for a salon by name or service.
  2. Open the salon profile and tap Book an Appointment.
  3. Choose a service, staff member (if applicable), and an available time slot.
  4. Review your booking summary and confirm payment.
  5. You'll receive a confirmation notification immediately.
How do I reschedule or cancel a booking?

Go to Appointments (bottom nav), find the booking, and tap Reschedule or Cancel.

Cancellation policies vary by salon. Check the salon's policy on their profile page. Late cancellations may incur a fee as set by the individual salon.

I didn't receive a booking confirmation — what should I do?
  • Check your Appointments tab in the app — your booking will appear there if it was successful.
  • Check your email spam folder for the confirmation email.
  • Make sure notifications are enabled for Zarkili in your device Settings.
  • If the booking doesn't appear in the app, contact support@zarkili.com.

Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

Zarkili accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe. Apple Pay is supported on compatible devices. Cards are tokenised and stored securely — your full card number is never stored by Zarkili.

How do I add or remove a payment method?
  • Go to Profile → Payment Methods
  • Tap Add Card to save a new card
  • Swipe left on an existing card and tap Remove to delete it
I was charged but my appointment was cancelled — when will I be refunded?

Refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

If you believe you were incorrectly charged, contact support@zarkili.com with your booking reference number.

Loyalty & Rewards

How do I earn loyalty points?

Points are earned automatically every time you complete a paid booking at a salon that has loyalty enabled. The points rate is set by each individual salon. You can see your current points balance in the Rewards tab.

How do I redeem a reward?
  1. Go to the Rewards tab and tap a reward you want to redeem.
  2. Confirm the redemption — points will be deducted immediately.
  3. Present the reward code to the salon at your next visit, or it will be applied automatically at checkout.
Do my points expire?

Point expiry is controlled by each salon's loyalty programme policy. Check the loyalty programme details on the individual salon's profile, or ask the salon directly via the in-app messaging feature.

Notifications

How do I turn off notifications?

On iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Zarkili → toggle off Allow Notifications.

To manage individual notification types (reminders vs. promotions), go to Zarkili → Profile → Notification Settings.

For Salons

How do I list my salon on Zarkili?

Salon onboarding is handled separately through the Zarkili Business app. Contact us at hello@zarkili.com to get started. We'll walk you through setup, service configuration, and going live on the marketplace.

Does Zarkili charge commission on bookings?

No. Zarkili operates on a flat monthly subscription model for salons. There are no per-booking commission fees. Your clients are your clients — Zarkili will never redirect them to competitor salons.
