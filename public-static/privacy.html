Privacy Policy

Zarkili  ·  Effective date: May 11, 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Welcome to Zarkili ("the App", "we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights regarding your data.

Zarkili is operated by Mobendo.
By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

Data Controller: Mobendo

Product: Zarkili — salon discovery, booking and loyalty platform

Contact: privacy@zarkili.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account details: first name, last name, email address, phone number
  • Profile photo: optional image you upload from your camera or photo library
  • Payment information: card details — processed and stored securely by Stripe; we never store raw card numbers
  • Booking information: services requested, dates, times, notes, and selected salon
  • Messages: content of messages you send to or receive from salons
  • Reviews: ratings and written reviews you submit

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage data: screens visited, actions taken, time spent, search queries
  • Crash and performance data: error logs collected via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Push notification tokens: used only to deliver notifications you have opted into

2.3 Location information

We request access to your approximate or precise location while the app is in use (foreground only) for the purpose of showing you salons near you on the discovery map. We do not collect your location in the background. Location data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

2.4 Camera and Photo Library

We request access to your camera and photo library only when you choose to upload a profile photo or when a salon owner uploads salon images. The app does not access your camera or photos at any other time.

4. Sharing Your Information

4.1 With salons you book

When you make a booking, the relevant salon receives your name, phone number, email address, selected services, and appointment time. This is necessary to fulfil the booking. The clients you generate through Zarkili belong to that salon — we do not re-market them to other salons.

4.3 We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to any third party for advertising or commercial purposes.

4.4 Legal disclosures

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or when necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of Zarkili, our users, or the public.

5. Push Notifications

We send push notifications for booking confirmations, reminders, cancellations, messages from salons, loyalty reward updates, and (with your explicit consent) promotional offers from salons you follow.

You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's Settings → Notifications → Zarkili. Turning off notifications does not affect your ability to use the app or make bookings.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data retained for the lifetime of the account
  • Deleted accounts: personal data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion request; anonymised booking statistics may be retained for analytics
  • Transaction records: booking and payment records may be retained for up to 7 years to fulfil legal and accounting obligations
  • Messages: retained until you or the salon deletes the conversation

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against accidental loss, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS for all communications)
  • Encryption at rest for all data stored in Firebase
  • Stripe PCI-DSS compliance for payment data
  • Firebase Security Rules restricting data access to authorised users only
  • Regular security reviews and access control audits

Despite these measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at security@zarkili.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Zarkili is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at privacy@zarkili.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and associated personal data
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent at any time (e.g. notifications, location)
  • Restriction: request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@zarkili.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Slovenia, this is the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec): www.ip-rs.si.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States, where our service providers (Firebase/Google, Stripe, OpenAI) operate. These transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

11. Cookies and Tracking

The Zarkili mobile app does not use browser cookies. We use Firebase Analytics SDK for in-app analytics, which may use a resettable device identifier. You can reset this identifier in your device settings.

The Zarkili web version (if applicable) may use session cookies strictly necessary for authentication. No third-party advertising cookies are used.

12. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services (such as salon websites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you via a push notification or a prominent notice in the App, and update the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
